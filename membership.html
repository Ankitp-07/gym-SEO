Membership FAQs

Can I upgrade my plan mid-way?
Yes, you can upgrade from Monthly to Quarterly or Yearly at any time. We will pro-rate the remaining days of your current plan and adjust the cost accordingly.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm), credit/debit cards and net banking. We do not charge any transaction fees.
Is the joining fee refundable?
The one-time joining fee of Rs 500 is non-refundable. However, your membership fee is refundable within 7 days of joining if you are not satisfied with our services.
Can I transfer my membership to someone else?
Memberships are non-transferable. Each membership is tied to a single individual and cannot be shared or gifted to another person.
Are personal training sessions extra?
Yes, beyond the complimentary sessions included in your plan, additional PT sessions are available at Rs 500 per session or Rs 4,000 for a package of 10 sessions.
