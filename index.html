Frequently Asked Questions

What are your gym timings?
We are open Monday to Saturday from 5:00 AM to 10:00 PM, and Sunday from 6:00 AM to 12:00 PM.
Do you offer a free trial?
Yes! We offer a free 1-day trial pass so you can experience our facilities and meet our trainers before committing to a membership.
Is there a joining fee?
There is a one-time joining fee of Rs 500 for new members. This is waived during special promotional periods.
Do you have separate batches for ladies?
Yes, we have dedicated ladies-only batches in the morning from 7:00 AM to 9:00 AM and in the evening from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM.
Can I pause my membership if I travel?
Yes, members on quarterly and yearly plans can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per year with prior notice.
Do you provide lockers?
Yes, complimentary lockers are available for all members. Separate locker rooms for men and women.
